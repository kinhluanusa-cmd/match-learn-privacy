Match & Learn Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

SilverHat ("we," "our," or "us") provides Match & Learn, an educational matching game designed for young children. We take children's privacy seriously and aim to minimize the collection and use of personal information.

Summary for Parents

Match & Learn does not require children to create an account, provide their name, email address, phone number, or other contact information. The app does not include social networking, chat, or user-generated content.

1. Information You Provide

Match & Learn does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to play the game.

2. Device and Technical Information

The Google Play version of Match & Learn may use third-party services necessary to provide app functionality, advertising, purchases, security, and technical operation. These services may process limited device or technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable Google Play requirements.

3. Children's Privacy

Match & Learn is designed for young children. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information through the app.

Where advertising is provided, the app is intended to use child-appropriate advertising settings and services consistent with applicable Google Play Families requirements. We do not intentionally use personalized advertising for children.

4. Advertising

The free Google Play version of Match & Learn may display limited advertising. Advertising is intended to be placed only in parent-controlled areas and not during children's matching-game rounds or immediately next to child-focused game controls.

The app may use Google AdMob to provide advertising. Advertising functionality is configured with the intention of complying with Google Play requirements applicable to apps directed to children.

5. Premium Purchase

Match & Learn may offer an optional one-time Premium purchase through Google Play. Premium removes advertising and may provide access to additional premium functionality.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not directly receive or store users' payment card or banking information.

6. Third-Party Services

The Google Play version may use services provided by Google, including:

  • Google Play services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services, prevent fraud, maintain security, process purchases, and operate advertising, subject to their respective policies and applicable requirements.

7. Permissions and Sensitive Features

Match & Learn does not require children to provide access to features such as the camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location for normal gameplay.

8. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell children's personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the Google Play version of the app, as described above.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Match & Learn does not provide user accounts or directly request personal information from children, the app does not maintain a user-account database from which a child needs to request account deletion.

Information processed independently by third-party services is subject to the retention and deletion practices of those service providers.

10. Offline Edition

A separate offline edition of Match & Learn may be distributed independently of the Google Play edition. The offline edition is designed to operate without advertising or Google Play Billing.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features, or applicable requirements change. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when appropriate.
