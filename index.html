Match & Learn Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

SilverHat (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides Match & Learn, an educational animal-matching and alphabet-learning game designed for toddlers and young children. We take children’s privacy seriously and design the app to minimize the collection and use of personal information.

Summary for Parents

Match & Learn does not require children to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, precise location, or other contact information.

The app does not include chat, social networking, public profiles, behavioral tracking features, or user-generated content.

The Google Play version may use Google Play Billing and limited, child-directed advertising in a protected parent area. The separate Offline edition does not include advertising or Google Play Billing.

1. App Features and Versions

Match & Learn includes three educational activities:

  • Head to Body
  • ABC Animals
  • Drag & Match

ABC Animals provides alphabet-learning activities from A–Z. The free Google Play version includes eight regular animals. An optional Premium purchase unlocks the complete collection of 40 regular animals, removes advertising, and includes future Premium animals and learning modes.

A separate Offline edition includes all 40 regular animals and does not contain advertising or Google Play Billing.

2. Information Provided by Users

Match & Learn does not require users to create an account or directly provide personal information to play.

Children are not asked to submit:

  • Their name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Precise location
  • Photographs
  • Audio recordings
  • Contacts
  • Account credentials

Parents or guardians may voluntarily contact us by email. We use information submitted in a support request only to respond to the request, resolve problems, and maintain appropriate business records.

3. Learning Progress Stored on the Device

Match & Learn may save limited gameplay information locally on the user’s device, such as ABC Animals progress, unlocked content, settings, and Premium entitlement status.

This information is used to remember progress and app preferences. It is not used to create a child profile or for personalized advertising.

Locally stored information can generally be removed by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app. Purchase records may remain associated with the purchaser’s Google Play account so that eligible purchases can be restored.

4. Device and Technical Information

The Google Play version may use third-party services needed for app distribution, advertising, purchases, fraud prevention, security, diagnostics, and technical operation.

Depending on how these services operate, they may process limited technical information, such as:

  • Device and app information
  • General diagnostic information
  • App interactions
  • Advertising interactions
  • Purchase and transaction status
  • Approximate region derived from network information
  • Information used for fraud prevention and security

We do not use this information to create independent behavioral profiles of children.

5. Children’s Privacy

Match & Learn is designed for toddlers and young children. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information through the app.

The Google Play version is intended to use child-directed settings and services consistent with applicable Google Play Families requirements. We do not intentionally serve personalized or interest-based advertising to children.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us using the information below. We will review the request and take appropriate action.

6. Advertising

The free Google Play version of Match & Learn may display limited advertising through Google AdMob.

Advertising is intended to:

  • Appear only in a protected parent area
  • Remain outside children’s gameplay rounds
  • Avoid placement next to child-focused game controls
  • Use child-directed and non-personalized advertising settings
  • Follow applicable Google Play Families requirements

Purchasing Premium removes advertising from the Google Play version.

The separate Offline edition does not contain advertising.

7. Premium Purchase

Match & Learn may offer an optional one-time Premium purchase through Google Play Billing.

Premium unlocks all 40 regular animals, removes advertising, and includes future Premium animals and learning modes.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not directly receive or store payment-card or banking information. Google may process purchase information as necessary to complete transactions, prevent fraud, provide receipts, and restore eligible purchases.

8. Third-Party Services

The Google Play version may use services provided by Google, including:

  • Google Play services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob

These providers may process information as needed to operate their services, process purchases, display child-directed advertising, maintain security, prevent fraud, and provide technical support.

Their processing is governed by their respective terms and privacy policies.

View Google’s Privacy Policy

9. Permissions and Sensitive Features

Match & Learn does not require access to the following sensitive device features for normal gameplay:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • Precise location
  • Photos or videos
  • Files belonging to other apps

The Google Play version may access the Internet for advertising, purchase processing, purchase restoration, Google Play services, security, or related technical functions.

The separate Offline edition is designed to operate without Internet access and does not include advertising or Google Play Billing.

10. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell children’s personal information.

Limited technical or transaction information may be processed by service providers used to operate the Google Play version, as described in this policy. We do not authorize those providers to use children’s information for personalized advertising.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect users, investigate fraud or security issues, or enforce legal rights.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Match & Learn does not maintain user accounts or a child-profile database.

Gameplay progress stored locally remains on the device until it is deleted through the app, the app’s data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

Purchase records and information independently processed by Google are subject to Google’s retention and deletion practices.

Support emails may be retained only as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, maintain records, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations.

Parents and guardians may contact us with privacy questions or deletion requests involving information submitted directly to us.

12. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information under our control. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Because Match & Learn does not require child accounts or direct submission of personal information, the amount of information maintained directly by us is limited.

13. International Users

Match & Learn may be available in multiple countries. Information processed by Google or other service providers may be handled in countries outside the user’s place of residence, subject to those providers’ policies and applicable legal protections.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Match & Learn, its features, third-party services, or applicable requirements change.

When the policy is updated, the effective date at the top of this page will also be updated. Material changes may be communicated through the app, its store listing, or this webpage when appropriate.
